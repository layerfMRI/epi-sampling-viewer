EPI k-space sampling: CAIPI & segmentation

Each square is one (ky, kz) phase-encode position. Colored squares are acquired; the color shows the segment (shot within a kz block). Hover or click a sample to highlight every line acquired in the same shot, in echo order.

Trajectory:

Pattern notation S·(Ry×Rz)zΔ. Within a shot the echo train runs along ky; kz blips are b(1) = (S·Δz) mod Rz and −b(2) = −(Rz−b(1)) mod Rz, repeating every nS echoes. Successive kz blocks (Rz partitions each) are acquired by further excitations. Shot numbering (block-major) is for illustration; the actual loop order is vendor-specific. Based on Stirnberg & Stöcker, MRM 2020, doi:10.1002/mrm.28486.
